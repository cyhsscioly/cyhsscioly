Why Sponsor Us?

Science Olympiad gives students the opportunity to take science beyond the classroom. Through hands-on experimentation, engineering, research, and competition, our team develops skills that last far beyond tournament day.

Your sponsorship helps provide the materials, equipment, travel, and resources our students need throughout the season.

Your support helps fund

  • Laboratory supplies and chemicals
  • Engineering and construction materials
  • Scientific equipment and instrumentation
  • Tournament registration and competition expenses
  • Travel to competitions
  • Team merchandise and competition materials
  • Resources for practices and student preparation
ImportantEvery contribution makes a difference

Whether you are a local business, organization, family, or individual, your support directly contributes to opportunities for Central York students.
